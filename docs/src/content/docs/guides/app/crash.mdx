**Why:** “the app closes when I tap Buy” is a bug report developers can't do much with. The stack trace from the
device tells them the file and the line. Here you get it in two clicks.

<Guide id="crash" />

## What happens

**Logcat** is Android's own log: apps and the system write into it what they do, and a crash leaves its full
error there.

1. The app <Hint to="phone-buy" step={1}>crashes</Hint>: Android shows “keeps stopping” on the screen.
2. Holtdroid notices it and shows a <Hint to="toast" step={2}>notification</Hint> with the first lines of the
   error. Press <Hint to="toast-action" step={3}>Logcat</Hint> in it.
3. The Logcat tab opens. <Hint to="log-exception" step={4}>Red lines</Hint> are errors: the exception name, its
   message and the lines starting with `at` show where in the code it happened.
4. Narrow it down: <Hint to="log-scope" step={5}>This app / Everything</Hint> switches between your app's lines
   and the whole device, the <Hint to="log-level" step={6}>level</Hint> filter keeps only errors, and the text
   field finds a tag or a word.
5. <Hint to="log-download" step={7}>Download</Hint>
   saves what is shown, ready to attach to the ticket.
6. The app stays closed after a crash. <Hint to="app-restart" step={9}>Restart app</Hint> on the App card brings it
   back.

## Good to know

- The log is collected from the moment the device is ready, and survives app restarts and installing another
  build. You can find a crash that happened ten minutes ago.
- **This app** follows your app by its user id, so it covers all of its processes and keeps working after
  restarts.
- For a bug report with everything at once (screenshot, log, device details), use the **Bundle** button on the
  Device card.

## If it doesn't work

- **No notification appeared**: the app may have closed without a crash, for example it called `finish()` or was
  killed for memory. Look at Logcat with **Everything** and the **Error** level.
- **The Logcat tab is missing or says it's waiting**: the tab works over the device's ADB, which isn't available
  to you in someone else's session.
- **The error is in the web part of the app** (a white screen, a broken button): look in the
  [DevTools console](/holtdroid/guides/web/devtools/) instead; JavaScript errors don't crash Android apps.

More details: [Logcat](/holtdroid/session-tools/logcat/), [ADB tools](/holtdroid/concepts/adb/).
