**Why:** you test the checkout every day, and every day you sign in, confirm a code and skip five onboarding
screens first. Save that once and start from it.

<Guide id="app-data" />

## What happens

**App data** is what the app keeps on the device for itself: who is signed in, settings, databases, the WebView's
cookies and local storage.

1. Get the app into the state you want: signed in, onboarding done, a test cart filled.
2. Press <Hint to="app-save" step={2}>Save app data</Hint> on the App card, or simply <Hint to="stop" step={3}>Stop</Hint>
   the session: stopping saves the data too.
3. Next time, in the launch dialog, pick your <Hint to="data-saved" step={7}>save</Hint> instead of
   **Fresh install**. Tick <Hint to="data-cache" step={8}>Also restore the cache</Hint> if you want cached
   files back as well.
4. The progress list shows <Hint step={10}>Restoring app data</Hint>, and the app <Hint to="phone" step={11}>opens where you left it</Hint>.

## Good to know

- Saves are **yours only**: nobody else sees them, admins included. The **App data** page in the header lists
  them; you can download or delete them there.
- Only a few recent saves per app are kept, and saves nobody used for a while are deleted (by default 6 saves
  and 7 days).
- The quick launch button (the lightning icon) can start straight from your newest save.
- A save from a **newer build** may not work in an older one, for example when its database can't be
  downgraded. The launch dialog warns you.

## If it doesn't work

- **There is no App data section in the launch dialog**: saving is turned off on this server. Ask whoever runs
  Holtdroid.
- **The app signs you out anyway**: some apps encrypt their data with keys that stay on the original device
  (Android Keystore). Such data can't move to another device; this is a limit of Android, not of the save.
- **Stop takes long**: it waits for the save to finish. A big app with a big cache saves longer.

More details: [App data](/concepts/app-data/).
