**Why:** “the button looks a bit off” is hard to argue about. Laying the mockup over the real screen shows exactly
what is off and by how many pixels, and the report settles it.

<Guide id="pixel-perfect" />

## What happens

1. Open the <Hint to="tab-pixel-perfect" step={1}>Pixel perfect</Hint> tab next to DevTools.
2. <Hint to="pp-drop" step={2}>
     Add the mockup
   </Hint>
   : drop an image, choose a file or paste it with Cmd+V (Ctrl+V). It becomes a
   <Hint to="pp-ref" step={3}>
     reference
   </Hint>
   and lies over the
   <Hint to="phone" step={4}>
     screen
   </Hint>
   .
3. Use <Hint to="pp-opacity" step={5}>Opacity</Hint> to see more of the app or more of the design.
4. Press <Hint to="pp-compare" step={6}>Find differences</Hint>. Each one gets a
   <Hint to="pp-item-0" step={7}>
     plain explanation
   </Hint>
   : shifted, another colour, missing.
5. Click a difference to <Hint to="pp-issue-1" step={8}>find it on the screen</Hint>.
6. <Hint to="pp-export" step={10}>
     Export report
   </Hint>
   saves a picture to attach to the task.

## Good to know

- From Figma, SVG is the best format: select the frame and **Copy as → Copy as SVG**, then paste it into the tab. It
  stays sharp at any size. Keep the frame the size of the phone screen in dp (for example 360 × 800). If a frame
  can't be SVG, export PNG at 3×.
- References belong to you, not to the session: they are there in every session, the current app's first.
- The phone keeps working under the mockup: taps go through it.
- Something on the screen is gone in a second (a toast, an animation)? **Freeze** keeps the current picture so you
  can compare it calmly.
- A blend called **Difference** turns matching pixels black and lights up what is off: a quick check by eye.

## If it doesn't work

- **The mockup is blurry or the wrong size**: it was probably a PNG at 1×. Use SVG, or PNG at 3×.
- **Everything is a difference**: the mockup isn't lined up. Turn on **Move**, drag it into place, then compare again.
- **A video or map always differs**: hide that part of the mockup or skip that area of the screen in the tab, so it
  isn't compared.

More details: [Pixel perfect](/holtdroid/concepts/pixel-perfect/).
