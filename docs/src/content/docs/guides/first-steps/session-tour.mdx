## What's where

1. **The header** is about the session: the <Hint to="version-switcher" step={1}>app and its build</Hint> (click
   it to install another build), the <Hint to="state-badge" step={2}>state badge</Hint>, the <Hint to="session-timer" step={3}>time left</Hint>, and <Hint to="share" step={4}>Share</Hint>, <Hint to="extend" step={5}>Extend</Hint>
   and <Hint to="stop" step={6}>Stop</Hint>.
2. **The cards on the left** are the device's <Hint to="widgets" step={7}>quick settings</Hint>. Hover one to open
   its <Hint to="w-network" step={8}>panel</Hint>: change the country, the network, the GPS position, the battery,
   the language or the clock.
3. **The phone** in the middle is the <Hint to="phone" step={9}>real screen</Hint>. Its <Hint to="phone-buttons" step={10}>side keys</Hint>
   work like the hardware ones.
4. **The tools on the right** are tabs: <Hint to="tab-devtools" step={11}>DevTools</Hint>, WebSockets, <Hint to="tab-logcat" step={12}>Logcat</Hint>, Files, Terminal and Pixel perfect.

## Good to know

- A small amber dot on a card means that setting differs from the default, so you can see at a glance what you
  changed.
- Right-click the screen to wake the device when its display is off.
- If the app closes or crashes, it stays closed; the play button in the app card starts it again.
- Some cards and tabs only appear when your server turns the feature on, so your page may have fewer of them.

## If it doesn't work

- **The screen stays on “Connecting to the screen…”**: reload the page. If it keeps happening, try a recent
  Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- **Share, Extend and Stop are greyed out**: the session belongs to someone else. You can watch it, but only its
  owner and admins can manage it.

More details: [Sessions and slots](/concepts/sessions/).
