thing instead of hunting for it mid-test.

## What's where

1. **The header** is about the session: the <Hint to="version-switcher" step={1}>app and its build</Hint> (click
   it to install another build) with the device, Android, WebView and country under it, the
   <Hint to="state-badge" step={2}>state badge</Hint>, the <Hint to="session-timer" step={3}>time left</Hint>,
   <Hint to="extend" step={4}>Extend</Hint> and <Hint to="stop" step={5}>Stop</Hint>.
2. **The Device panel** on the left holds the device's <Hint to="device-panel" step={6}>controls</Hint> in three
   tabs: Environment, Media and App. A row's chevron opens it in place, like the
   <Hint to="w-network" step={7}>Network row</Hint>: change the country, the network, the GPS position, the
   battery, the language or the clock.
3. **The screen** in the middle is the <Hint to="phone" step={8}>real device</Hint>. The
   <Hint to="stage-toolbar" step={9}>toolbar</Hint> beside it has Back, Home, Recent apps, rotation, a screenshot,
   volume, power and Restart app.
4. **The tools on the right** are tabs: <Hint to="tab-devtools" step={10}>DevTools</Hint>, <Hint to="tab-logcat" step={11}>Logcat</Hint>, DNS, Routes, Files and Terminal, with WebSockets, Cache and Pixel perfect under **More**.

## Good to know

- A row marked **Changed** differs from the default, and its tab gets a dot, so you can see at a glance what you
  changed.
- Right-click the screen to wake the device when its display is off.
- If the app closes or crashes, it stays closed; **Reopen** on the card over the screen starts it again.
- Some rows and tabs only appear when your server turns the feature on, so your page may have fewer of them.

## If it doesn't work

- **The screen stays on “Connecting to the screen…”**: reload the page. If it keeps happening, try a recent
  Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- **Extend and Stop are greyed out**: the session belongs to someone else. You can watch it, but only its
  owner and admins can manage it.

More details: [Sessions and slots](/concepts/sessions/).
