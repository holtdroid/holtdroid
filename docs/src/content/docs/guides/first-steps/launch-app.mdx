**Why:** to try a build without installing it on your own phone, on exactly the Android version and screen
you need, and to throw the device away when you're done.

<Guide id="launch-app" />

## What happens

1. On the <Hint to="app-card">Apps page</Hint> every app is a row with its newest build. Press <Hint to="launch" step={2}>Launch</Hint>.
   The arrow at the end of the row opens its other builds, each with its own launch button.
2. At the top of the dialog, choose <Hint step={3}>where it runs</Hint>: a **virtual device** (Android in a
   container, any version) or a **physical phone** connected over USB; each shows how many are free. The
   <Hint step={4}>preview</Hint> on the right draws the phone you're putting together and sums up your choices.
3. Set up the device: <Hint step={5}>Android version</Hint>, <Hint step={6}>screen</Hint>, <Hint step={7}>WebView</Hint>, <Hint step={8}>country</Hint> and <Hint step={9}>app data</Hint>. The defaults are
   sensible: if you don't know what to pick, leave them.
4. Press <Hint step={11}>Launch</Hint> and wait for <Hint to="state-badge" step={13}>Ready</Hint>. It usually
   takes less than a minute.

## Good to know

- Every launch is a **clean device**: nothing from the previous session is left on it, unless you start from
  saved [app data](/guides/app/app-data/).
- The device is yours alone until you press **Stop** or leave it idle; then it goes back to the pool.
- The VPN location, network, language and other settings can be changed **during** the session, so you don't
  need to relaunch to try another country.

## If it doesn't work

- **Launch says “Join the queue”**: all devices are busy. Stay on the page: the device starts as soon as one
  frees up.
- **An Android version is greyed out**: the app doesn't support it (its `minSdk` is higher). Hover it to see why.
- **A provider or phone is greyed out with “Doesn't run this build”**: the build has native code only for another
  processor (an x86 build on an ARM phone, say) and there's no build of this version for the device's.
- **The device failed to start**: the reason is shown on the progress card. Launch again; if it repeats, send the
  message to whoever runs Holtdroid.
