**Why:** devices are shared. Knowing when one is taken back saves you from losing a half-reproduced bug when you
come back from lunch.

<Guide id="limits" />

## What happens

1. <Hint to="sessions" step={1}>Active sessions</Hint>
   on the Apps page lists every running device, and the <Hint to="slots" step={2}>bar above it</Hint>
   shows the pool: green devices are free, amber ones are busy.
2. Each card shows <Hint to="idle-timer" step={3}>how long until the device stops</Hint>. Two clocks run at once:
   - **idle time**: a device nobody uses is stopped after 30 minutes by default;
   - **maximum lifetime**: every device is stopped after 8 hours by default, used or not.
3. <Hint to="session-extend" step={4}>Extend</Hint>
   restarts the idle clock. It's also in the <Hint to="session-timer" step={7}>session header</Hint>.
4. When you're done, press <Hint to="stop" step={8}>Stop</Hint>: the device goes back to the pool right away.

## What counts as using the device

- clicks, typing, scrolling or touches anywhere on the session page, while the tab is visible;
- changing a setting, switching the location, restarting the app, pressing a phone key, using the clipboard;
- pressing **Extend**.

A tab that is just open in the background does **not** keep the device alive.

## Good to know

- Your server may use other values than 30 minutes and 8 hours; the countdowns always show the real ones.
- Your server may limit how many devices one person runs at once. Your limit is in **Account and passkeys** in the
  user menu.
- When the pool is full, a launch waits in line and starts as soon as a device frees up, as long as you stay on its
  page.

## If it doesn't work

- **“You already run as many devices as you may; stop one first”**: you hit your limit. Stop a device you no longer
  need.
- **The device disappeared while you were away**: it was idle too long. Next time press Extend before you leave,
  and [save the app data](/holtdroid/guides/app/app-data/) if signing in again is tedious.
- **Extend and Stop are greyed out**: it's someone else's session; only its owner and admins can manage it.

More details: [Activity and reaping](/holtdroid/concepts/sessions/#activity-and-reaping).
