**Why:** a user from Kazakhstan says checkout shows the wrong price, and you are in the Netherlands. Switch the
device to Kazakhstan and see exactly what they see.

<Guide id="vpn" />

## What happens

1. The <Hint to="w-network" step={1}>Network card</Hint> shows the current country and the device's public IP.
2. Hover it to open the panel and pick another country under <Hint step={4}>VPN location</Hint>. The switch takes
   about a second; the app keeps running.
3. Refresh the screen in the app and check what changed, for example the <Hint to="phone-price" step={6}>prices</Hint>.
4. To see what the server answered, open <Hint step={8}>the request in DevTools</Hint>.

## Good to know

- **All** of the device's traffic goes through the chosen country: every app, and DNS lookups too, so nothing
  gives the real location away.
- The country is also picked in the launch dialog; the one you used last time is remembered.
- The VPN changes the IP address only. GPS, language and the time zone are separate: set them in their own cards
  if the app checks them (see [GPS location](/guides/conditions/gps/) and
  [Language, time, theme and battery](/guides/conditions/device-settings/)).
- Sessions on the same country share its IP address.

## If it doesn't work

- **There is no VPN location in the panel**: this Holtdroid has no VPN configured. Ask whoever runs it.
- **The app still shows the old country**: many apps detect the region once and remember it. Restart the app, or
  launch with a fresh install.
- **The switch spins and fails**: that location is unreachable right now. Pick another one or try again later.

More details: [Per-session VPN](/concepts/vpn/).
