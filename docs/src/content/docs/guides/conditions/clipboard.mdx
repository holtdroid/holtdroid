**Why:** a 30-character promo code, a test card from the wiki, a link the app generated for sharing. Typing them on
a phone is slow and error-prone; copying is instant.

<Guide id="clipboard" />

## What happens

1. The <Hint to="w-clipboard" step={1}>Clipboard card</Hint> shows whether sync is on and in which direction. A
   click on the card turns it on or off.
2. Hover it to choose the direction:
   - <Hint to="clip-both" step={3}>
       Both ways
     </Hint>
     : copy on either side, paste on the other;
   - <Hint to="clip-to" step={4}>
       To the device
     </Hint>
     : only what you copy on the computer goes to the phone;
   - <Hint to="clip-from" step={5}>
       From the device
     </Hint>
     : only what the app copies comes to your computer.
3. To paste, tap a field on the phone and press <Hint to="phone-search" step={6}>Ctrl+V or Cmd+V</Hint>.

## Good to know

- Ctrl+V or Cmd+V on the screen **always** pastes, even with sync off. Sync is needed for the other direction and
  for the app's own Paste button.
- The panel keeps a short history of what went back and forth in this browser tab, with buttons to copy an entry
  again or save it as a text file.
- Pasted text goes into the device's clipboard quietly, without Android's clipboard preview popping up.
- Only the session's owner gets clipboard sync: someone watching a shared session doesn't receive your clipboard.

## If it doesn't work

- **Nothing reaches the device on its own**: the browser doesn't let the page read your clipboard. Allow clipboard
  access for the portal in the browser, or just press Ctrl+V or Cmd+V on the screen.
- **There is no Clipboard card**: you are watching someone else's session, or this device doesn't support it.
- **The app's copy doesn't arrive**: check that the direction is Both ways or From the device.

More details: [Sessions and slots](/holtdroid/concepts/sessions/#the-app-inside-a-session).
