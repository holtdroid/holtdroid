**Why:** in the office everything is fast, but users open the app on the train. Check that it shows loaders,
retries and clear errors instead of a blank screen or a double payment.

<Guide id="network" />

## What happens

1. Open the <Hint to="w-network" step={1}>Network card</Hint>. Its **Network conditions** row leads to the
   **Network** tab next to DevTools, with every network the session can emulate.
2. Pick a named network such as <Hint step={3}>3G</Hint>. Each one shows its delay, bandwidth and loss and a short
   description.
3. Use the app and watch <Hint to="phone" step={4}>how it behaves</Hint>: slow images, late prices, timeouts.
4. Scenarios like <Hint step={5}>Subway</Hint>, Driving or Elevator change the network over time, with short
   periods of no signal.
5. <Hint to="airplane" step={8}>Airplane mode</Hint>, Wi-Fi and Mobile data turn the network off completely.
6. Pick <Hint step={9}>No emulation</Hint> to get the normal connection back.

## Good to know

- Only the apps' traffic is slowed down. The screen, DevTools and ADB stay fast, so you can still work comfortably.
- During an outage in a scenario Android still thinks it is online: requests hang until they time out, like on a
  dead cell. Airplane mode is different: Android knows the network is gone and apps are told so.
- Regions add the typical round trip of users in a country to the CDN and to your servers. Combine them with a
  [VPN location](/holtdroid/guides/conditions/vpn/) of the same country.
- The numbers are realistic averages from public reports, not measurements of a specific operator.

## If it doesn't work

- **There is no Network conditions row**: emulated networks need VPN locations; this Holtdroid has none configured.
- **The app is faster than the chosen network promises**: the device's VPN route is already slower than that
  network. The Network tab warns about it; pick a closer VPN location.
- **Everything is slow, including the screen**: that is your own connection, not the emulation.

More details: [Network conditions](/holtdroid/configuration/emulator/#network-conditions).
