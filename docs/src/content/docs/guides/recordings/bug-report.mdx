**Why:** a ticket that says “the button doesn't work” comes back with “which phone, which Android, which build,
any logs?”. A bundle answers all of that before anyone asks.

<Guide id="bug-report" />

## What happens

1. Open the <Hint to="w-device" step={2}>Device row</Hint> on the App tab of the Device panel. It shows the model,
   the Android version and the build.
2. Press <Hint to="bugreport-bundle" step={3}>Bundle</Hint>. A few seconds later a zip is downloaded: a screenshot,
   the logcat collected so far, and the device and app details.
3. Attach the zip to the ticket.

## Good to know

- Logcat is the phone's system log: what the app and Android wrote while you were testing. Crashes and errors
  land there, which is why developers always ask for it.
- **Full report** next to Bundle runs Android's own `bugreport`. It is much bigger and slower; use it only when a
  developer asks for it.
- To keep the evidence after you stop, or to show it to someone without an account,
  [record the session](/guides/recordings/record-share/) and share the recording.

## If it doesn't work

- **Bundle is greyed out or the row says ADB is unavailable**: the browser couldn't connect to the device's ADB
  (the Android debugging channel). Wait a few seconds after the device starts; on some portals ADB is turned off.

More details: [ADB tools](/concepts/adb/).
