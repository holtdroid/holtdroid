**Why:** a bug showed up once and you're not sure you can repeat it. With a recording, the developer sees the
screen, the logs and every request of that very run, instead of reading “it sometimes fails at checkout”.

<Guide id="record-share" />

## What happens

1. While a session is recorded, the <Hint to="w-recording" step={1}>Recording card</Hint> next to the screen shows
   the elapsed time.
2. When something goes wrong, <Hint step={2}>click the card</Hint> or press
   <Hint to="rec-mark" step={4}>
     Mark this moment
   </Hint>
   : a marker is pinned on the recording's timeline.
3. Open <Hint to="nav-recordings" step={5}>Recordings</Hint> in the header and pick the recording. It plays like a
   video, with logcat, the WebView's network and console moving along with the picture.
4. <Hint step={8}>Click a marker</Hint> to jump to it, and look at the <Hint step={9}>Network</Hint> tab for the failed
   request.
5. Press <Hint to="rec-share" step={10}>Share</Hint>: a link is copied. Paste it into the ticket.

## Good to know

- Depending on how your portal is set up, sessions are recorded **always**, **unless you turn it off** in the
  launch dialog, or **only when you turn it on**. If the card says “Not recording”, click it to start.
- A recording keeps more than the picture: logcat (the device's system log), the WebViews' requests, console and
  WebSockets, the device, network and load, and your taps. Typed text is never recorded.
- A share link opens the recording **without signing in** and carries the moment you're at, so the reader lands
  right on the bug. Shared recordings and recordings with markers are not deleted automatically.
- Very short recordings (under 20 seconds by default) are thrown away when they end.

## If it doesn't work

- **No Recording card or no Recordings button**: recordings are turned off on this portal. Ask whoever runs it.
- **Share is missing**: share links are turned off on the portal; download the recording file instead
  (**Download → Recording file**).
- **The recording ends early**: it ends when the session stops. Stopping and starting it again makes a new
  recording.

More details: [Recordings](/holtdroid/concepts/recordings/).
