**Why:** chats, live prices and order statuses arrive over WebSockets, not ordinary requests, so the Network list
doesn't show them. This tab does, and shows the exact message that came before a bug.

<Guide id="websockets" />

## What happens

1. Open the <Hint to="tab-websockets" step={1}>WebSockets</Hint> tab. A **WebSocket** is a connection that stays
   open, so the server can send updates whenever it wants.
2. Each line is <Hint to="frame-hello" step={2}>one message</Hint>: an arrow up was sent by the app, an arrow down
   came from the server, with its length and time.
3. Click a message, such as the <Hint to="frame-price" step={3}>price update</Hint>, to read it
   <Hint to="frame-details" step={4}>
     in full
   </Hint>
   .
4. Look for the message right before things went wrong: here the
   <Hint to="frame-order" step={5}>
     order
   </Hint>
   failed with `out_of_stock`, and the app never told the user.

## Good to know

- The tab works without opening DevTools.
- Holtdroid keeps the session's socket traffic from the moment the app started, so the tab shows it all even after
  you reload the portal page or open the session on another computer.
- **Clear** empties the list in your browser only. **Hide under 15 B** leaves out pings and other tiny messages.
  Pause stops the list; resuming fills in what came meanwhile.
- Event streams (another way servers push updates) show up here too.
- While the session is recorded, click a message's time to put a marker on the recording at that moment.

## If it doesn't work

- **The list is empty**: the page hasn't opened a socket yet. Open the part of the app with live data.
- **A socket is marked as video and has no messages**: video streams are left out on purpose, they would flood the
  list.

More details: [WebView DevTools](/holtdroid/concepts/devtools/).
